    How it works
    
  
    
    Press Start and hold a relaxed face for about two seconds while it learns what
    your neutral looks like. After that, smile, frown, raise an eyebrow, or try a
    thumbs up, a peace sign, an open palm, a fist, or pointing. Your expression is
    matched to a real cat or dog reaction with a caption. Press c to recalibrate
    and s to save a snapshot.
  
    
  
    
    Daily challenge gives everyone the same five prompts each day, three faces and
    two hand signs. Hold each one for under a second before the six second clock runs
    out. Your first run of the day counts; replays are practice.
  
    

  
    Privacy
    
  
    
    Your camera feed is processed entirely in your browser and is never uploaded,
    recorded, or stored. Snapshots are saved only to your own device. To show
    reactions, the page loads images from Giphy, The Cat API, or Dog CEO, and the
    face and hand runtimes from jsDelivr and Google's model storage. Giphy receives a
    short search phrase for your current mood, such as "happy cat". Like any website,
    these services see your IP address when they send a file. This site sets no cookies.
    Daily challenge scores and your streak are kept only in this browser's storage, and
    a share image includes your face only if you tick the box.
  
    

  
    Also available as a desktop app
