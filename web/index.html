What it does

It watches your face and hands, works out your mood, and shows a real cat or dog reaction next to your webcam with a caption. Give it a second to calibrate when you start, then pull a face. Every reaction is a real photo fetched live, never generated or drawn.

Privacy

Everything runs inside this page on your own machine. The video never leaves your device and nothing is recorded or uploaded. The only network requests are to fetch the cat and dog photos.

Also available as a desktop app

The original version runs locally in Python with a few extra features, including mood matched reactions and generated captions when you supply your own keys. Source and downloads on GitHub.
